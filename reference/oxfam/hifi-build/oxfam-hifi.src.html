Oxfam Support Redesign

Restructuring Oxfam Australia’s support pages and the MyOxfam portal so supporters can actually find help — led by research, validated with real users.

My role

Led UX research and design end-to-end — supporter research and a persona, information architecture, wireframes, high-fidelity visual design, and usability testing.

Outcome

A clearer, user-tested route through Oxfam’s support content and a restructured MyOxfam portal — presented to stakeholders. See results ↓

Problem overview

Oxfam Australia supports Indigenous and impoverished communities across Australia and neighbouring countries. As support content grew, the pages and the MyOxfam portal became hard to navigate.

Supporters couldn’t reliably find answers or manage their giving — friction in an experience that should feel effortless and trustworthy. [Tighten with your exact framing.]

Supporters were getting lost on the way to help — and to their own account.

Design decisions

Research pointed to a clearer information architecture and a support flow organised around real questions, not internal structure.

  • StructureReorganised support & MyOxfam content around supporter intent
  • WayfindingSix clear entry points from one contact landing
  • PortalRestructured MyOxfam so account tasks are easy to find
  • AccessAccessible, legible, brand-aligned patterns
