Media Contacts

Lily Partland

Based in Melbourne, Lily is Oxfam Australia’s Head of Media.

The media team covers all areas of Oxfam’s work and seeks to inform the public about our overseas aid and development programs, our response to humanitarian disasters and our advocacy work on issues such as inequality, labour rights, First Peoples’ rights, and the impacts of climate change in low-income countries.

Mobile: +61 418 118 687

Email: Lilyp@oxfam.org.au

Lucy Brown

Based in Melbourne, Lucy is Oxfam Australia’s Senior Media Coordinator.

Prior to joining the aid sector, Lucy has worked in media and communications for the National Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Legal Services and Vice Media.
