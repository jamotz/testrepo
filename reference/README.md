# reference/ — your source material

Drop your existing site's **screenshots, page images, and flow diagrams** here.
Claude uses these to rebuild your case studies and prototypes. Nothing here is
published to your live site directly — it's the staging area Claude pulls from.

## How to upload (GitHub website — no tools needed)
1. Open the folder you want (e.g. `reference/oxfam/page-01`).
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
3. **Drag your images in**, then click **Commit changes** (green button).

That's it. Tell Claude once you've uploaded and it'll take it from there.

- `origins/` — the app project (bubble graph + filters + screens)
