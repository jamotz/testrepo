About Us

Origins is a Washington cannabis retailer built around a simple idea: the plant makes more sense when someone explains it. Our shelves are organised the way people actually shop — by how you want to feel, not by the chemistry on the label.

Every product carries its lifestyle, its cannabinoids and its real numbers up front, and Origins University is there whenever you want the longer answer. No jargon, no upsell — just a shop where the first-time buyer and the daily regular can both find their footing.
