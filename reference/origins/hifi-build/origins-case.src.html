Origins App Redesign

Redesigning a cannabis dispensary’s app from behind the counter — real customers, real inventory, and filters that finally return the right products.

My role

Led UX research and design end-to-end from inside the store — competitive SWOT, in-person surveys and interviews, personas, user-flow and IA analysis, lo-fi through hi-fi design, and usability testing at the counter.

Outcome

A leveled filter system that can’t return wrong results, a reorganized shop and home, and 20+ hi-fi frames — presented in person to the shareholders and the COO. See results ↓

02

Problem overview

Origins is a local cannabis shop I started working at in July of 2022 — they specialize in helping people who are new to cannabis, at every age. The shareholders and COO asked for help understanding why customers struggled with their app.

Client briefing

  • 01Make the app look cleaner and flow better with in-store operations to increase sales
  • 02Understand why users are having technical issues with the app
  • 03Bring more interaction to the store — even when it isn’t about buying product
  • 04Create better marketing for the app

Identifying the issues

The store doesn’t clearly or consistently display product information. Pricing is often wrong — sale prices show as a dollar off, or contradict the posted deal. Menus and filters need reorganizing. The shopping cart exceeds the proportions of the screen and doesn’t display pricing correctly. The blog and messages serve little purpose and could be removed, replaced, or reimagined.

Proposed solution

Take user feedback and create an interface that displays information properly while keeping an organized appearance. Make sure the inventory, server, and in-house systems are properly connected so information stays correct. Add a new section that creates real interaction with the app — education and reviews.

I wasn’t guessing at the pain points — I rang them up at the register every day.

Research approach

I researched competitor websites of local shops as well as the top-performing store in the state to gather insight into content layout and overall structure — then went straight to the people at the counter.

Competitive market analysis — SWOT

Strengths

What makes our app unique — what functions better than other competitors?

Weaknesses

What makes our app function and look worse than our competitors?

Opportunities

What can we do to get ahead of our competitors using new innovations?

Threats

What do the competitors do better than our store?

User interviews — in person

I conducted individual interviews with a variety of customers, gathering quantitative data through user surveys and distinguishing between active users and non-users of the app.

User personas

I developed personas representing each key age group, which helped communicate to the shareholders the unique needs and preferences of each demographic.

04

Design decisions

Wireframes

I began by sketching the content layout in pencil, experimenting with the placement of page functions. The process was guided by insights from user research and analysis of competitor designs.

User flow analysis

Mapping the first-time user flow let me identify key pain points and present potential solutions to the shareholders — and illustrate how each issue could derail a user’s experience, making its significance easier to convey.

Information architecture

The information architecture had several issues — the major concern being that many filters either led to no products or returned incorrect products. Filters pointed at things the system couldn’t serve: categories with no inventory behind them, ratios no one could explain, and a “gear” bucket full of unlabeled product.

07

The redesign

Refining the information architecture and user flow was essential in reimagining the new set of menus and pages to seamlessly align with the app’s needs.

A leveled filter system

Level 1 — Basic

Indica ↔ Sativa spectrum mapped to plain feelings (sedative body high → euphoric & creative), size, form of consumption, and brand. Enough for anyone to shop confidently.

Level 2 — Advanced

Per-form depth: flower by grow (indoor, greenhouse, outdoor), concentrates by process (distillate, live resin, rosin), pre-rolls by build (traditional, infused, blunt).

Holistic & CBD

A dedicated CBD path with real ratios (20:1 → 1:1), topicals by use (cooling, warming, soothing) — and vape clearly flagged as web-only instead of silently missing.

09

Reflection

Working the counter while redesigning the app was the unfair advantage.

Every assumption got tested within hours, not weeks — the people I designed for were standing in front of me. The biggest lesson: fix the fundamentals before the fancy stuff. Filters that return the right products beat any new feature.

Next time I’d push to instrument the app so improvements show up as numbers — search success, filter dead-ends, checkout completion — and I’d pilot the terpene education system I proposed once the basics landed. [Make it yours.]
